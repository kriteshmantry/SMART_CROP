ODISHA AGRICULTURE DATASET FIX REPORT
========================================
All original uploaded CSVs were left untouched. Fixed versions are separate.

MASTER REFERENCES
- district_master.csv: canonical district_id/district_name
- crop_master.csv: canonical crop_id/crop_name
- season_master.csv: canonical season IDs; SEA04 means multi-season Kharif+Rabi.

KEY CORRECTIONS
1. District names synchronized to geography master.
2. Crop IDs in production and agronomy were remapped by canonical crop name to resolve conflicting IDs.
3. Production season remains Annual; it is not falsely converted to Kharif/Rabi.
4. Production yield is recalculated as production_tonnes / area_harvested_ha.
5. Weather lag and rolling features were recalculated using only prior observations. Initial unavailable windows are NA, not zero.
6. Weather geographic fields synchronized to the district geography master.
7. Calendar was reduced to unique crop-season state-level reference records because the source explicitly says it is not district-specific.
8. Market prices were canonicalized and marked synthetic prototype; no fake historical claims are removed.
9. Crop observations remain synthetic; original coordinates were preserved in original_latitude/original_longitude and canonical district reference coordinates are used in latitude/longitude to avoid false geographic alignment.
10. Agricultural inputs remain district aggregate data. They were NOT expanded to every crop because that would fabricate crop-specific fertilizer values.

ML WARNING
- These datasets contain synthetic prototype data where the source/status says synthetic.
- They are suitable for pipeline/model prototyping, not real-world performance claims.
- Crop recommendation ground truth is not genuine farm-survey ground truth.
